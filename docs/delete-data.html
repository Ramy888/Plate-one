Delete your data

Last updated 10 September 2026

Everything is deleted from inside the app, in one tap. There is no account to close: almost everything Plate One knows is already on your own device. Settings → Delete my data erases it, and tells the server to forget the anonymous device id too. Nothing needs to be emailed and nobody needs to approve it.

Delete everything, right now

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. One tap and a confirmation. It erases everything on the device and tells the server to forget the anonymous device id, its allowance, its call records and any reports you sent. It is not reversible.

Other options:

  • Delete individual saved patches — open Saved patches and tap the × on any entry.
  • Uninstall — removes everything stored on the device. It does not remove the device record on our server; use Settings → Delete my data first if you want that gone too.

There is no row here for an email address, a name or a purchase record, because none of those exist. The photo you scan is sent to Google’s Gemini API to be described and is not stored by Plate One.

If you have already uninstalled

The in-app control is the fast way, and it needs the app. If Plate One is already gone from your device, the record left behind is an anonymous id with a call count against it and no way to link it back to you — it is swept automatically once the device stops being seen. If you would like it removed sooner, get in touch at the address on the project’s repository.
